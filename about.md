# About

*The best interiors don't feel unfamiliar. They simply show you what your space was always capable of becoming.*

*I always thought I fell into interior design by accident. It turns out, it was fate.*

Ocotillo Interiors is a full-service interior design studio rooted in storytelling, intuition, and deeply personal spaces. With over a decade of experience, my journey began in Orange County, where I studied, worked, and learned under remarkable designers who taught me not only how to refine my craft, but how to imagine what could be. That path led me into interior project management, overseeing residential and commercial projects throughout Los Angeles and Seattle, before relocating full-time to New Orleans to serve as Director of Design, guiding teams and projects across Louisiana, California, Washington, Texas, and beyond.

Eventually, life carried me home to California sooner than expected. I found my footing in the Coachella Valley, where Ocotillo Interiors took root, renovating homes into spaces designed for living fully, gathering often, and creating the memories that linger long after the last light is turned off. Over the years, I have overseen projects throughout Southern California, Washington, Texas, Mississippi, Louisiana, Illinois, and North Carolina, each one shaped by its people, place, and purpose.

Long before any formal training, though, I was decorating alongside my grandmother, Joyce Mae. Every holiday known to the calendar had a home in her house, fully realized, thoughtfully layered, and always ready to celebrate. That early sense of ritual, warmth, and personalization still defines my approach today.

With a strong belief in individuality, Ocotillo Interiors takes the time to listen and truly understand each client's story, lifestyle, and vision. The result is design that feels collected, intentional, and deeply personal. Spaces that do not push you out of your comfort zone, but gently expand it, revealing beauty, possibility, and magic you may not have known was there.
